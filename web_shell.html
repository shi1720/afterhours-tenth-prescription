Someone is still waiting.

A receipt prints your name beside ten unfinished prescriptions. Inside the abandoned pharmacy, your sister's records tell a story the official archive tried to erase.

AFTERHOURS is a compact, complete horror story about the weight of being forgotten. Its shadows belong to the supernatural world. Its pharmacy is fictional.

Atmospheric horror with jump scares, darkness, pursuit and light effects. Headphones are optional. In-game settings include gentler difficulty, high visibility and reduced atmospheric effects.

Stay for the story. Return when you need to.
