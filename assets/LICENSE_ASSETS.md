# AFTERHOURS asset provenance

These raster tiles, animation sheets, title illustration, icon, logo and sound recordings were created specifically for AFTERHOURS: The Tenth Prescription by original procedural drawing and sound synthesis. No third-party stock art, copied game sprites, sampled recordings or generative image sources were used.

Copyright © 2026 Shivam Gupta. All rights reserved. The game owner may use, modify, redistribute and commercially exploit these commissioned project assets.

Art: deliberately restricted ink navy, teal, pale mint, coral and warm amber palette. Images use hard pixel edges; title scene is authored at 320 × 180 and scaled 2× with nearest-neighbor sampling.

Audio: original sine-wave, modulation and seeded-noise synthesis at 22,050 Hz, mono 16-bit PCM WAV. Peaks are limited below full scale. Ambience is a 24-second loop with periodic tonal layers.

Sprites: player.png is 96 × 128, 32-pixel cells, three columns (walk frame 0, idle frame 1, walk frame 2), four rows (down, left, right, up). enemy.png is 128 × 32, four frames at 32 × 32. All gameplay tiles and pickups are 32 × 32. title_art.png is 640 × 360. icon.png is 128 × 128. asset_preview.png is for development review only.
