# Independent game-jam review

Review based on source inspection, the 4,945-assertion native headless suite, ten deterministic active-enemy objective routes, and direct inspection of the captured title/gameplay images. These are **review judgments**, not an official competition rubric or external judge score. No official scored rubric was supplied.

| Inferred criterion | Score / 10 | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|
| Requirement fulfillment | 9.0 | Godot implementation, pixel art, ten authored wards, Korean/English, tutorial, isolated demo and progression are present. Web deployment requires its own browser acceptance evidence. |
| Visual presentation | 8.0 | Consistent mint/coral palette, legible layout, strong title composition and cohesive custom pixel assets. Gameplay remains a compact top-down tile scene; muted sidebar text could be stronger. |
| UX and accessibility | 8.5 | Six-page skippable onboarding, context prompts, pause, quiet movement, gentle mode, high visibility and local saves. Keyboard/mouse experience is clear from implementation. No mobile-control claim is warranted. |
| Mechanical coherence | 8.0 | Charge now serves both story revelation and emergency defense, making resource use more meaningful. Enemy paths, hiding, hazard timing and charger noise interact coherently. |
| Originality and narrative | 7.5 | A pharmacist recovering forgotten names is an accessible, specific premise. Pulse-to-reveal records strengthens it. Core top-down pursuit, collection and hiding remain familiar genre mechanics. |
| Technical robustness | 8.5 | Deterministic simulation and state handling pass meaningful integration checks, including save isolation and corrupted values. Browser, audio and long-session compatibility are separate checks. |
| Commercial plausibility | 7.0 | A short browser demo and paid expanded atmospheric campaign are plausible. Current route length and limited mechanic variety need human playtesting before pricing or demand claims. No market validation has been performed. |
| Submission clarity | 8.0 | The premise and repeatable loop are easy to demonstrate. Honest credits, source, tests, walkthrough assets and transparent limitations improve credibility. Final recorded narration remains a human deliverable. |

**Overall review: approximately 8 / 10 as a compact game-jam release candidate.** There is no credible basis to guarantee a win or claim commercial demand from automated checks.

Highest remaining value: real first-player observation and exported-browser acceptance testing. An exact-knowledge pilot completes individual wards in approximately 12–16 seconds; this does not predict a novice's completion time but indicates that extended paid-game positioning would need substantially more content, uncertainty or evolving decisions. Avoid advertising an unmeasured playtime.

The final screenshots show a deliberately dark game. High-visibility mode is a sensible mitigation; verify it with players who find darkness difficult. A native Korean speaker should review tone and naturalness before a commercial Korean-language launch.
