# Product review criteria

This is an internal review framework, not an organizer score or independent human endorsement. The revised release needs fresh execution and play observations. Do not carry forward an earlier numerical score as evidence of current quality.

| Criterion | Product expectation | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creativity | A haunted pharmacy with a coherent reason for every objective | Player can explain the premise after play |
| Horror | Readable danger, escalation and earned relief | Observe tension and identify unfair surprises |
| Ten rooms | Distinct routes and meaningful survival decisions | Complete each room and compare encounters |
| Pixel art | Consistent palette, readable silhouettes and deliberate scale | Inspect screenshots at real desktop and touch sizes |
| Interface | Clear actions, state and resource feedback | First player proceeds without spoken coaching |
| Completeness | Onboarding, progression, retry, saves and endings work | Current native and browser evidence |
| Touch | Simultaneous movement/actions and no stuck controls | Actual or explicitly identified emulated-device testing |
| Accessibility | Visibility and reduced-effects controls preserve useful feedback | Check every warning with settings enabled |
| Commercial fit | A finite experience that players would recommend | Actual play study and later pricing research |

High priorities remain sustained encounter pressure, meaningful cover, recovery vulnerability, responsive touch usability and honest playtime. A perfect-knowledge route test demonstrates solvability, not enjoyment or appropriate difficulty. Use `RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` for current execution status.
